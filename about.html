About Us

What is Utsavika?

Utsavika is a small website where college students can find campus events, read what each event is about, and register for it in a few steps. It is made for students who miss events just because they never heard about them.

What you can do here

  • Browse events like Tech Fest, Colours of Culture and Champions Arena.
  • Read the date, place and activities of each event.
  • Register and get a ticket with a booking ID.
  • Keep a simple student profile and see your events on a dashboard.

Why we built it

Event news on campus usually travels through group chats and posters. Things get lost. We wanted one place where every event is listed clearly, so nobody has to ask "when is it and where?" again.
