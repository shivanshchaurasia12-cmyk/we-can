Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This page explains what information Utsavika (we-can.onrender.com) collects, why, and what choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Information you give us

When you register for an event, sign up, or fill in your student profile, you may enter details such as your name, email, phone number, college name, roll number or student ID, course, and event preferences. We use these only to run the site: to make your ticket, show your dashboard and answer your questions.

Payments

The payment page on this site is a demo. No real money is taken, and we do not want your real card number, CVV or UPI PIN. Please do not enter real payment secrets on it.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners show ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more at how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Do we sell your data?

No. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Sharing

We share information only when the law asks us to, or with service providers needed to run the site (for example our hosting provider and Google for ads).

Your choices

  • You can ask us to delete the details you gave us.
  • You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings.
  • You can choose not to fill optional fields in the profile.

Children

This site is meant for college students and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from young children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
